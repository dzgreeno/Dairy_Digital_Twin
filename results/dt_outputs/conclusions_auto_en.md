Separate individual losses (S2: 1.33% + S3: 4.11% = 5.44%), while simultaneous compound loss = 5.94% ← **Synergistic systemic effect: compound loss exceeds linear summation.** Spoilage alone surged to 4.02% (vs 1.33% in S2 and 0.59% in S3).

- **Logistics Breakpoint:** Systemic loss exceeds nominal baseline by >2.0 percentage points when transport delay multiplier reaches ≈ 2.6×.
- **Thermal Breakpoint:** Spoilage doubles compared to non-heatwave conditions at ambient heat delta ≈ +3°C (with accompanying cooling and grid failures).
